Transaction demo

A stand-in driver, {{ demo['driver_name'] }}, is signed by {{ team['team_name'] }} for {{ season }} (car {{ car }}) and gets a result in the {{ race['race_name'] }}. Three inserts have to succeed together:

  1. add the driver,
  2. create their season entry,
  3. record their race result.

If one step failed on its own, you could be left with a driver who has no entry, or a result that points at something that does not exist. Wrapping the steps in BEGIN … COMMIT/ROLLBACK makes them all-or-nothing. This page runs the same scenario as transaction.sql.
